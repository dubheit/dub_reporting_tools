Why Carbone

Your finance team designs the invoice, not you

QWeb is powerful but asks business users to read HTML. Carbone flips the relationship: anyone who knows Word or Excel can author a template, tag fields with {d.name}, and Carbone renders them back with real data — preserving every font, border and layout pixel.

Pick "Carbone" as the report engine, upload your template, and your existing ir.actions.report just works.

What's inside

The full Carbone API, wired into Odoo

Office-first authoring

Design templates in Word, Excel, LibreOffice or Google Docs. No HTML, no XML.

Multiple formats

Output PDF, DOCX, XLSX, ODT, ODS — pick what your customer expects.

Template caching

Each template is hashed once; subsequent renders reuse it. Zero wasted uploads.

Async rendering

Large reports render in the background and notify Odoo via webhook. No HTTP timeouts.

Carbone Studio

Live, in-browser template editor with Odoo data bindings preloaded (Enterprise licence).

Translations

Localise content per language using the Carbone i18n engine, bound to Odoo's lang.

Example

Plain Word, with tags

Hello {d.partner_id.name}, Your invoice {d.name} for {d.amount_total:formatC(EUR)} is due on {d.invoice_date_due:formatD(DD/MM/YYYY)}. {d.invoice_line_ids[i].name} {d.invoice_line_ids[i].price_subtotal} {d.invoice_line_ids[i+1].name} {d.invoice_line_ids[i+1].price_subtotal}
