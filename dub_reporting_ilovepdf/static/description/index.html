Turn any attachment into a PDF workflow

Every PDF or Office attachment in Odoo gains a Process with iLovePDF action. Pick an operation, tweak its options, and the processed file comes back attached to the same record — chatter message included.

Works on any ir.attachment: invoices, quotations, purchase orders, tickets, custom modules. If Odoo can attach it, iLovePDF can process it.

Operations

Thirteen tools for the price of one module

Office ↔ PDF

DOCX/XLSX/PPTX to PDF, and PDF back to DOCX or XLSX. Also HTML → PDF.

Image ↔ PDF

Merge images into a single PDF, or rasterise PDF pages to JPG / PNG.

Compress

Three compression levels: low, recommended, extreme. Trade quality for size.

Merge & split

Combine multiple PDFs or split one into page ranges or fixed-size chunks.

Rotate

Fix scanned pages quickly. Per-page or whole-document rotation.

Watermark & page numbers

Text or image watermarks with position, opacity and rotation. Custom-positioned page numbers.

Protect / unlock

Add or remove passwords; control print, copy and edit permissions.

OCR

Turn a scan into searchable text in any of 50+ languages.

Repair & PDF/A

Fix damaged PDFs, or convert to archival-grade PDF/A for compliance.
